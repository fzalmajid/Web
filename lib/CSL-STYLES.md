# Vendored citation styles

`cslStyles.json` contains independent CSL styles from the official
[Citation Style Language styles repository](https://github.com/citation-style-language/styles):
APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago 18 author-date, University of Leeds Harvard, and IEEE.
Downloaded 2026-10-01. The original style XML includes its authors, contributors,
source links, and Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license.
Vancouver is bundled by @citation-js/plugin-csl.

These styles are loaded locally. Citation generation never downloads a style,
calls an AI model, or requires a Mendeley account. Preserve the embedded attribution
and license when modifying or redistributing the styles.
